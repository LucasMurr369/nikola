## What the God Code Is (and Isn't)

**It IS:**
- A daily quote that cycles in your header (every ~5 min, or every scan).
- An **adversity quote** that fires exactly when a stop-loss hits — the moment you're tempted to panic.
- A **level-up quote** when you bank your first TP1, hit a milestone, or reach a new XP level.
- An **ethical veto** — Nikola will *refuse* profit from harming others (forced liquidations, misery arbitrage).

**It ISN'T:**
- A market timer. It doesn't time trades — it steadies the trader *during* them.
- Exclusive. It draws from **all old religions** — because truth, wherever found, is from God.
- Pushy. No notifications. No spam. No sharing of your trades unless you opt in.

> *"The unexamined trade is not worth making."* — Socrates, via Nikola

The God Code turns the trading desk into a **meditation**. Every 15 seconds, the market refreshes and a proverb reminds you to breathe.

## Trading History: A Timeline of Lessons

Each era taught one thing — and each lesson lives in Nikola's code today.

```
📜 1637 — Tulip Mania (Netherlands)
    Lesson: "This too shall pass."
    → Nikola's SL2 (-4.4%) is the bulb that didn't pop your whole account.

📜 1602 — Dutch East India Co. (first joint-stock, first margin)
    Lesson: Diversification across voyages.
    → Nikola: MAX_OPEN_POSITIONS=0 (budget gate, no count cap) + MARGIN_TIERS (≤8% per pair): never all eggs in one trade.

📜 1860s — Telegraph ticker (Edwin Bell)
    Lesson: Real-time data changes everything.
    → Nikola's 15s light cycle: price now, not yesterday.

📜 1970s — Portfolio insurance (Black Monday '87)
    Lesson: Dynamic hedging fails in gaps.
    → Nikola's BTC cascade guard: when correlation hits 1, scale out.

📜 1975 — Turtle Traders (Richard Dennis)
    Lesson: Simple rules + discipline beat genius.
    → Nikola's fixed TP1/TP2/SL geometry: no "I know better" buttons.

📜 1980s — Index futures + LTCM
    Lesson: Leverage amplifies wisdom AND folly.
    → Nikola's 3:1 R/R entry gate: only take it if the math is clean.

📜 2008 — Financial crisis
    Lesson: Correlations go to 1 in panic.
    → Margin reserve 25%: survive the storm, not just the trade.

📜 2010s — Algo trading proliferation
    Lesson: Speed without wisdom is suicide.
    → The God Code: the algo that remembers you're human.

📜 Today — Crypto leverage, 24/7
    Lesson: Sleep is a competitive advantage.
    → Nikola's cycle_sec=300s: breathe every 5 minutes, or you'll bleed.
```

**The pattern: every generation invents a sharper knife. The survivor is the one who keeps a steady hand.**

## Why It's Math — and Why That's Good News

Nikola does what he does **because the math demands it** — and the math is on your side *if you let it*.

```
Expectancy = (Win% × Avg Win) − (Loss% × Avg Loss)

Nikola targets:
  Win%  ≈ 50%     (high, not heroic)
  R/R   ≥ 3:1     (minimum; expands with MTF confirmation)
  Loss  ≤ -2.1%   (SL1 partial; -4.4% hard cap SL2)
  Win   ≥ +1.4%   (TP1 banked fast)

=> Expectancy ≈ (0.50 × 1.4) − (0.50 × 2.1) = +0.35% per trade (before compounding)
```

**That's positive expectancy at a coin-flip win rate.** You don't need to be right more than half the time — you need to be **right-sized** when you're wrong.

### The 3:1 gate is the guardrail
Below 3:1 R/R, slippage + fees + emotion erode you over 100 trades. Nikola's entry veto (`strategy.py`) walks away unless the geometry clears. **Rejection is a feature, not a bug.** Most losses happen on trades that shouldn't have been taken.

### Breakeven is freedom
At +0.1R, the BE ratchet arms. After TP1 + TP2 fill, SL snaps to **+2% floor**. So once you've banked half your profit, *the remaining position cannot lose money.* That is mathematical freedom: you're trading with the house's money.

### Leverage amplifies — but only of the good
3–5x on a 3:1 setup = ~15% return on margin if TP1 hits. On a $300 paper account, that's $45 → $345 → reinvested. **Margin done right compounds.** Margin done wrong (no stops, no gate) implodes.
